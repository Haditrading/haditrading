Forex Trading Bots & Expert Advisors (EAs)

🔹 Trading Bots

  • General Term: "Bot" refers to any automated software that trades markets like Forex, stocks, and crypto.
  • AI & Algorithmic Trading: Some bots use AI, machine learning, or complex algorithms.
  • Multi-Platform Use: Bots can run on MT4, MT5, cTrader, or custom software.

🔹 Expert Advisors (EAs)

  • MetaTrader-Specific: Designed for MT4/MT5.
  • Rule-Based Trading: Executes trades based on programmed strategies.
  • No Emotions: Removes human emotions like fear and greed.

🔹 Key Differences

Feature Trading Bots Expert Advisors (EAs)
Platform Multiple (MT4, MT5, cTrader, etc.) MetaTrader (MT4/MT5)
Flexibility Can use AI and adaptive strategies Uses fixed rule-based strategies
Customization Advanced with AI/ML options Traders can modify/create their own

🚀 Should You Use an EA or Bot?

  • If you trade on MT4/MT5, an EA is a great choice.
  • If you want more flexibility or AI-powered trading, look for a bot.
  • Always backtest and use a demo account before trading live.
